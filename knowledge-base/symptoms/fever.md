# Fever

Fever can happen with viral infections, bacterial infections, heat illness, inflammatory illness, or other causes.

Common discovery terms include fever, bukhar, high temperature, body pain, chills, and headache.

Common pharmacy discovery medicines include paracetamol brands such as Dolo, Crocin, Calpol, and Pacimol. This does not mean the bot is prescribing a dose.

Safety notes:

- For children, seniors, pregnancy, liver disease, very high fever, breathing difficulty, confusion, dehydration, or fever lasting more than two days, consult a doctor.
- Avoid duplicate paracetamol combinations.
- This bot helps discover medicines and is not a replacement for a doctor.
