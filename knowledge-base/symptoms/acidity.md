# Acidity And Gas

Acidity, gas, reflux, and heartburn are common pharmacy queries in India.

Common discovery salts include pantoprazole, omeprazole, and rabeprazole. Common brands include Pan 40, Pantocid, Omez, Ocid, Razo, and Rabicip.

Safety notes:

- Repeated acidity, chest pain, vomiting blood, black stools, unexplained weight loss, or severe stomach pain should be reviewed by a doctor.
- Long-term acidity medicine use should not be continued without medical advice.
